# Reference-image prompts (cast & environments)

Locked before production. Every generation was anchored on these.

## Gladias
Medium skin tone, short dark hair, clean-shaven, calm measured expression. Long ash-grey hooded robe, dark charcoal under-sleeves, dark diagonal chest strap, dark boots. Watch on LEFT wrist. Face covering, when worn, is the robe's own hood scarf — one continuous ash-grey fabric across the lower face, never a separate mask.

## Mov
Warm medium-brown skin, short curly dark hair, clean-shaven (never any facial hair). Burnt-orange knit sweater under a long light-grey open coat (never hooded), dark trousers, dark boots, dark crossbody satchel, dark watch on left wrist.

## Delwart
Pale gaunt face, receding grey-streaked dark hair, deep-set eyes. Long dark slate-grey hooded cloak, dark fingerless knit gloves, dark worn boots.

## Seila
Appears ONLY inside watch photos. Light olive skin, shoulder-length wavy dark hair, gentle closed-lip smile; dark charcoal wool coat over a dark green top.

## Service robot
Rounded white/grey wheeled service bot; domed head with dark faceplate showing an amber LED dot-matrix smiley; articulated arms with a flat serving tray; teal vertical side panels, purple accent stripe. Chirps and chimes only — never speech. Gentle brief comedy only (antenna wobbles, small tilts), never slapstick.

## Environments
- **Meldan shopping street:** sunlit pedestrian street, tiled pavement, florist stall, coat shop, amber tech kiosk.
- **Veridia tunnel:** curved white concrete underpass, green/purple wayfinding stripes, warm downlights, handrails.
- **GPH exterior wall:** brutalist grey stone facade, blank notice board, glass doorway with green access panel, tree-lined sidewalk in haze.
- **GPH interior:** white/grey buildings with purple accents, poplars, sunken amber ramp entrance.
- **GPH hidden courtyard booth:** vine-covered courtyard cafe, private wooden booth with amber lamp and purple flowers, curtained.
