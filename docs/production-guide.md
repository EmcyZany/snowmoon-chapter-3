SNOWMOON — CHAPTER 3 PRODUCTION GUIDE
"A Choice of Roles" (Meldan, Veridia · 3724 Snowmoon 8)
Format & CapCut settings (same as Chapter 2)
16:9 landscape. Final export: 1920 × 1080, 24 fps.
Flow: 16:9, highest practical resolution (1080p if available). One 8-second clip per scene.
Sound: generate ambience per scene in Flow; record all dialogue separately in CapCut for voice consistency.
Text: NEVER ask Flow to render readable posters, watch UIs, ads, or barcodes. Add all of them in CapCut as overlays.
Continuity: use Chapter 2's Fin/Zei only if you want a flashback Easter egg — Chapter 3 itself needs none of them.
Common visual prompt (paste at end of every prompt)
Visual continuity for this Snowmoon Chapter 3 adaptation: cinematic grounded science-fiction, believable human characters, restrained natural acting, soft atmospheric depth, realistic fabric and materials, warm green daylight outdoors with diffused tunnel lighting, clean civic order with subtle unease, subtle film grain, clear faces and hands. Controlled color palette of Veridian leaf green, civic glass-and-steel grey, Arctic accent purple, and warm amber practical lights. Keep faces, hairstyles, body proportions and clothing exactly consistent with reference images. No subtitles, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no legible generated text, no extra fingers, no duplicated people, no sudden costume changes. 16:9 widescreen composition.

STEP 3 — SCENE PROMPTS (in story order, ~8s each)
1. Meldan street — establish Veridia
Wide tracking shot following Gladias from behind as he walks along a Meldan sidewalk past small shops — flowers, clothing, electronics — warm green daylight, orderly and comfortable. He crosses a street. Cinematic calm, lived-in civic utopia with faint stillness beneath.
Audio: soft footsteps, distant gentle shop activity, birds, light traffic hum. No dialogue.
2. Into the tunnel
Camera follows Gladias down a sloping road that transitions seamlessly into a softly lit tunnel, just bright enough to read by. Slow push-in as he descends.
Audio: footsteps developing a soft tunnel echo, quiet ventilation hum. No dialogue.
3. The Arctic poster
Gladias stops at a Y-shaped intersection in front of a poster taped at its four corners. Camera slowly pushes in over his shoulder toward the blank poster. He tilts his head, unimpressed.
Dialogue (Gladias, dry, internal-musing tone): "Yay. More Arctic propaganda."
Audio: tunnel hush, faint air movement, paper texture as he considers it. Add the full Lord Ephelion poster text in CapCut as a slow push-in overlay. No other text in frame.
4. The tap on the shoulder
Gladias turns; Mov stands behind him in his casually worn robe, grinning.
Dialogue, Mov, deadpan: "No, it's the Arctic Emperor. I'm here to literally suck out your soul."
Dialogue, Gladias, flat: "Okay, fine…"
Audio: one soft footstep, tunnel ambience, a tiny chime as they register each other's presence.
5. How Mov found him
Two-shot, both robed, walking slowly through the tunnel.
Dialogue, Gladias: "I'm one of a hundred people in privacy robes. How did you find me?"
Dialogue, Mov: "You left me on your trust list. Our AIs enabled location sharing for today."
Dialogue, Gladias, wry: "Ah. Contextual intimacy, or whatever the new buzzword is."
Audio: footsteps, soft echo, faint robe fabric movement.
6. The API tax problem
Medium two-shot as they walk; Mov's expression turns serious.
Dialogue, Mov: "Bluewhale is cutting its API. Verified hardware only. They say spam — everyone says control."
Dialogue, Gladias: "And their algorithms?"
Dialogue, Mov: "Totally opaque. Already paying the max tax bracket for it."
Audio: tunnel ambience, low watch chirp. Add a Bluewhale-style abstract logo only in CapCut (invented, non-readable).
7. The Keeper who was exposed
Close on Mov, quiet and sober.
Dialogue, Mov: "One Keeper wasn't careful. Her views went public — and her husband lost his job days later."
Dialogue, Mov, quieter: "That's why we run everything through cryptography, random selection… and privacy robes."
Audio: footsteps fall softer here; a faint tense low tone begins under the scene. No music swell.
8. Flashback — Silverchat
Brief stylized flashback: a younger Gladias at a simple desk, warm light, typing; a minimalist chat interface glows (abstract, no readable text).
Dialogue, Gladias (V.O.): "I learned to program building an alternative client. The open API is what got me into Steering."
Audio: soft keyboard ticks, warm nostalgic tone, then a clean cut back to present tunnel. Note the palette shift (amber memory vs green present).
9. A fresh robe
Mov opens his robe, pulls out a neatly folded package, hands it over. Slight beat; his smile fades a little.
Dialogue, Mov: "Fresh privacy robe. The Arctics are getting better at smuggling surveillance in."
Dialogue, Gladias: "At this level, maybe we shouldn't meet at all."
Dialogue, Mov, sincerely: "You're a friend, Glad. I enjoy this — while we still can."
Audio: fabric rustle, package handoff, tunnel echo; the line "while we still can" lands with a beat of silence.
10. Light, trees, children
The tunnel slopes up; warm outdoor light blooms. Gladias passes two children at a water fountain, trees lining the path.
Dialogue, child one (distant, cheerful): "…went to Northglade with my family…"
Dialogue, child two (distant): "…their Helisport team is the best…"
Audio: fountain water, birds, children's voices soft and natural.
11. Seila's photos
Close-up: Gladias's watch buzzes. Show an abstract notification glow only. Cut to his face softening. He taps a reply.
On-screen (CapCut): Seila's message with 4 photo thumbnails — Freetown downtown, a park gate, and a close crop of a price-list poster on the left edge. His reply: a heart symbol.
Audio: gentle watch chime, a single soft tap.
12. The construction barriers
Gladias walks past tall construction barriers. Camera pans across: first a warm civic kindness poster, then a satirical Hydrafill ad — bottle graphic on the left, blank text panel on the right. Gladias smiles.
Audio: distant construction muffled behind barriers, street ambience.
CapCut overlays: poster 1 — "Be kind toward your families and your community." Poster 2 — the full Hydrafill hydrogen-hydroxide satire text, revealed line by line.
13. The castle wall and the GPH poster
Wide shot: the long grey-purple wall, two rows of trees, the large blank poster. Gladias stops, raises his watch, scans the 2D barcode in the corner.
On-screen (CapCut): the GPH Public Activities menu — Environment, Health, medicine, Education, Art, "open source" note, Quadratic Funding footer.
Audio: soft scan chirp, gentle UI ticks, leaves.
14. Overheard on the bench
Gladias pauses facing the poster; behind him two men on a bench share tea and a hand device.
Dialogue, man one: "Have you ever been in there?"
Dialogue, man two: "Never managed to get a visa. It's like a country inside a country."
Dialogue, Gladias, whispering, barely audible: "Emerald — what taxes do they pay?"
On-screen (CapCut): Emerald's reply card — "Sales tax ~5.2% · Land tax 1.48% · Graph & Quadratic Funding recover roughly half."
Audio: birds, tea cup clink, whisper-level processing chirp at his neck band.
15. "The worst we can do to them"
The second man gestures at the wall, frustrated but humane.
Dialogue, man two: "They do all this work — environment, education, museums — and people still pretend their wall is ugly just to push their land tax up."
Dialogue, man one: "At least here that's the worst we can do. In Devanvil or Inglewore, GPH would be fighting to survive."
Audio: street ambience low; Gladias listening, then walking on.
16. The gate
Gladias holds his watch near the green circle on the gate. One beat.
Dialogue, machine (robotic but approving): "Proof verified. Welcome."
Audio: soft verification tone, gate mechanism, a hush as he passes through — sound design should feel like crossing a border.
17. Inside Greater Plum Harbor
Slow glide down a GPH street: uniform white-grey-purple buildings, trees at exact equal spacing, small identical logos, glass and steel, sharp edges.
Dialogue, Gladias (V.O., quietly): "Beautiful and impressive. Not like Veridia. Not for everyone."
Audio: hushed orderly ambience, softer than the Veridian street — the sound itself is more controlled.
18. The crowd and the readings
Gladias descends into a busy tunnel space, pulling his robe's face cover strap to split it below the nose. Close on the watch.
On-screen (CapCut): CO₂ 965 · PM2.5 22.1 · Attested ✓ 55 · Unknown ? 6.
Dialogue, Gladias (V.O.): "Too many people. And six unattested devices. Could be a science project. Could be worse."
Audio: dense footsteps, muffled crowd murmur (no intelligible words), his slightly filtered breathing.
19. The courtyard booth
Gladias slips into a curtained booth in a hidden courtyard: decorated lamp, large flowers on each side, the white box in the corner. His watch chirps; he notices the PM reading drop. He removes his hood and taps the green circle on the table.
Audio: courtyard murmur softened by the curtain, one tap, confirmation tone, faint clean-air hum from the filter box.
20. The smiley robot
A small service robot with a smiley face slides up with water and a salad. Gladias takes them; the robot slides away.
Audio: smooth servo glide, tray set-down, a tiny cheerful electronic chirp.
21. "So we meet at last"
A robed man steps into the booth and sits. Slow push-in.
Dialogue, Delwart: "So. We meet at last."
Dialogue, Gladias, controlled: "You track me down, demand a meeting, and I don't even know your name."
Dialogue, Delwart: "Delwart. Pleased to meet you."
Audio: curtain swish, seating, room tone drops quieter — intimacy and tension.
22. Showing off
Delwart leans back, too comfortable.
Dialogue, Delwart: "I know your wife is Seila. Your children's names are—"
Dialogue, Gladias, cold: "Are you trying to intimidate me?"
Dialogue, Delwart, genuinely chastened: "No. That was showing off. I apologize."
Audio: quiet; both remove face covers and hoods; cups set down. Beat of silence after the apology.
23. The Dreadknot test
Both eat and drink. Delwart watches him.
Dialogue, Delwart: "Sentinels want Dreadknot pushed to Tier 4. You voted Tier 3. Most Acolytes cave. You didn't."
Dialogue, Gladias, guarded: "You were brave — that's rare."
Audio: soft fork and cup sounds, courtyard murmur far away.
24. The tax asymmetry
Dialogue, Delwart: "A well-considered argument about how GPH benefits Veridia is worth less than a barely thought-through 'I don't like you.'"
Dialogue, Gladias: "So what do you propose?"
Dialogue, Delwart, simply: "Freedom. Stop nudging everyone. Let people's true selves come out."
Audio: low room tone, a faint tension drone entering under "Freedom."
25. Delete every rubric
Two-shot, Delwart leaning forward now.
Dialogue, Delwart: "Be a Keeper. And just vote to delete every rubric you see."
Dialogue, Gladias, incredulous: "That's it? That's your plan?"
Dialogue, Delwart: "Sentinels get courted because they touch specific things. Keepers change the system itself."
Audio: quiet, measured; no music swell — let the words carry it.
26. Graph Funding and Dzego
Dialogue, Gladias: "The open tech Graph Funding supports helped Dzego defend itself. Isn't that important?"
Dialogue, Delwart: "We're playing a dangerous game, funding this close to war. Fix our own problems first."
Audio: pause after "first" — let the disagreement sit in silence.
27. Why listen to you
Close-up on Delwart, calm and certain.
Dialogue, Delwart: "I found you. I know how you think. Imagine what Bluewhale could do. Or the Arctics. I can protect you — but I need your help."
Dialogue, Delwart, standing: "The deadline is in a month. Don't be a Sentinel. Be a Keeper."
Audio: chair movement, curtain swish as he rises.
28. The follower
Gladias holds the curtain crack open, watching Delwart walk away. Just before Delwart disappears, a second robed figure exits the adjacent booth and follows him. Close on Gladias's eyes narrowing.
On-screen (CapCut): watch screen — "Gladias · Mov".
Audio: courtyard murmur, fading footsteps, one watch chime. End on held beat, then cut to black.

Voice consistency notes
GLADIAS: Adult male voice, calm mid-range, dry wit, measured and thoughtful, never theatrical; tension shows as quietness, not shouting.
MOV: Adult male voice, warm and quick, playful with a sincere undertone; the humor is deadpan.
DELWART: Older adult male voice, low, smooth, unhurried; charming and faintly menacing without ever raising volume.
MACHINE/ROBOT: Neutral synthetic voice, rounded and polite; robots chirp, they don't talk.
