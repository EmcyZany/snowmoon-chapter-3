# Tools and models

- **Video generation:** prompt-led AI video generation (~10s clips), anchored on locked character/environment reference images.
- **Dialogue audio:** baked into the generated clips by the video generator; extracted from the video files for the final edit.
- **Assembly:** ffmpeg for trims/concats/composites; CapCut for the final edit, text overlays, and watch-screen cards.
- **QC:** every take is frame-checked before delivery — faces vs. references, costume locks, no mixed roles, no legible generated text, correct speaker on camera.
