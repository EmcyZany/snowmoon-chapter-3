# Voice guide

Voices are baked into the video clips by the generator — there are no reusable voice IDs. The locked references:

- **Gladias** — calm mid-range adult male, dry wit, measured, never theatrical. Tension shows as quietness, not shouting. Forward reference: the baked-in Scene 5 v3 audio.
- **Mov** — warm, quick adult male, deadpan humor with a sincere undertone. Forward reference: the baked-in Scene 5 v3 audio.
- **Delwart** — low, smooth, unhurried older male; charming and faintly menacing; never raises his volume.
- **Service robot** — chirps and chimes only; never speech.
- **Seila** — appears only in watch photos; no voice.

Rules: one character, one voice, one line at a time — never mixed, blended, or swapped. Each character's line is performed only by that character, sequentially, with no overlap.

## Audio source

All dialogue and ambient audio used in the final edit was extracted from the video files themselves. No separate dialogue masters are kept in this repo.
